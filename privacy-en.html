Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · version 1.0

1. Data controller

Luca Canarecci, Paris (France) — fawrinparis@gmail.com. This policy is provided under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Data we process

Account: email, password (encrypted), name, nickname, profile photo, bio, birth year (optional).

Home: the address you set as "home" and its coordinates, used only on your device and in your profile to compute travel times. Never shown to other users.

Location: if you allow it, the device location to centre the map. Not stored on our servers.

Content: events, days, diary pages, thoughts, favourites, agenda, photos and videos you upload.

Messages: texts, photos, videos and voice notes sent in chats, visible only to participants.

Relationships: friendships, follows, blocks, participations, reports.

Subscription: Premium status, plan and expiry (from the stores via RevenueCat); we never receive your card details.

Technical data: session identifiers, access logs, device model and OS, needed for security and operation.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Providing the service (contract): account, map, agenda, chat, events, Premium.

Safety and moderation (legitimate interest and legal obligation): preventing abuse, handling reports and blocks.

Location and notifications (consent): revocable at any time in your device settings.

Service communications (contract): confirmation emails, password recovery, important notices.

We do not sell your data and do not run profile-based advertising.

4. Who sees what

Profile, nickname, photo, bio, created events, public days and published thoughts are visible to other users. Agenda, private diary, favourites and home address are visible only to you (past agenda may be visible to friends). Chats are visible only to their participants. People you block cannot see or contact you.

5. Processors

Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), servers in the European Union.

RevenueCat (subscriptions) and Apple App Store / Google Play (payments).

Expo (push notifications and updates).

API Adresse (data.gouv.fr) for address search; Wikimedia Commons for place photos.

Some processors may process data outside the EU with GDPR safeguards (standard contractual clauses).

6. Retention

Data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, profile, content, files and subscription are erased immediately; your messages in other people's chats become "Message deleted" with no author; technical backups are overwritten within 30 days. Reports may be kept anonymised for service safety.

7. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. You can edit or delete your account in the App (Profile → Settings) or write to fawrinparis@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or your local authority.

8. Children

The App is for people aged 16 and over. Accounts of younger people brought to our attention are deleted.

9. Security

Encrypted connections, encrypted passwords, per-user data access rules, private chat files with temporary links. No system is infallible: report any issue to fawrinparis@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when needed and notify you in the App of significant changes.
